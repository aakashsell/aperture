Is Aperture for you?

If you’re shipping software and wondering “is this safe?” or “is this better?”, yes. Start with one rollout or one experiment—without building a platform first.

01 / Ship carefully

Don’t make every user your first tester.

Put a new feature behind a gate. Start with 5% of installations, watch failures, and expand or roll back from one place.

02 / Build with agents

Make rollout part of your coding workflow.

Ask your agent to integrate Aperture. It wires up the gate and health signals; you review the change and approve each rollout step.

03 / Learn what works

Optimize the experience as you grow.

Test a clearer signup, better onboarding, or a new pricing page. See the evidence and uncertainty before deciding what to ship.

A bigger lift—or a broken experiment?

Your agent writes the test. Aperture checks its work.

Missing exposure events can make a variation look better than it is. Aperture checks assignment, exposure coverage, traffic balance, and the evidence behind the result.
